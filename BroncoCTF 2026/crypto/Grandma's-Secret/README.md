# Grandma's Secret

## Intended Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Grandma wants to protect her wifi password. Can you find out what it is before grandpa does?

## Author
dot.t

---

## Background
The ADFGVX cipher is a real historical cipher used by the German 
military during World War I. It combines substitution using a Polybius 
square with a columnar transposition, making it significantly harder 
to crack than either technique alone.

---

## Inspiration
I was inspired by my cryptography course (MATH 178) at Santa Clara University.
The ADFGVX cipher was one of the first historical ciphers I learned in that class and I thought it was so creative!

The letter format came from my time working at a stationery shop. I 
love old fashioned handwritten correspondence. The cipher made me think, 
who would use this today? Grandma trying to be sneaky...
