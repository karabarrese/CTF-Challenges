# Crowed Trip

## Intended Difficulty: Beginner

## Description
Our lovely mascot Bucky the Bronco went on a super fun road trip last summer! He started from his pedestal at SCU and drove east, passing through 2 places shown in the photos.

His buddy Craig the crow wants to visit those same places too and then fly back. Can you calculate the total round-trip distance for Craig "as the crow flies"?

Round to the nearest 25 miles.

## Background
OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) is the practice of collecting 
information from publicly available sources. Geolocating photos is 
a core OSINT skill, learning to identify locations from visual 
clues like skylines, signage, landmarks, and geography.

This challenge is designed to be AI-resistant! 
You'll need to actually look carefully at what's 
in each photo.

---

## Inspiration
I took a road trip to Michigan for an internship and thought the 
journey would make a great OSINT challenge. I wanted to create 
something that rewards careful observation over just throwing images 
into AI tools. Real OSINT is about noticing details others miss.
